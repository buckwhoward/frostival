Who we are

Frostival is a holiday event at Howell Family Farms in Arlington, Texas. Frostival and this website, frostivaltexas.com, are operated by Eastover, LLC dba Winter Festivals ("Frostival," "we," "us" or "our"). This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, join our email or text list, buy tickets or contact us, and how we use and protect it.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • List sign-ups: your first name, email address and, if you choose, your mobile phone number, along with your consent to receive emails or text messages.
  • Ticket purchases: tickets are sold through a third-party ticketing provider. That provider collects your name, contact details and payment information to complete your order and shares order details with us so we can manage your visit. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.
  • Messages to us: anything you send when you email us or reply to our messages, such as questions about tickets or exchanges.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical details such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages you view, collected through cookies and similar technologies (see Cookies & analytics).
  • Whether you open our emails or click their links, which helps us send fewer, better messages.

How we use it

  • To send the emails and texts you signed up for, such as ticket drops, opening-night news, offers and event updates.
  • To process and manage ticket orders, exchanges and weather rescheduling.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To understand how people use our website and improve it, our event and our marketing.
  • To keep our website and event safe and to meet legal obligations.

Email

When you join the Frostival list, we add your email address to our mailing list, managed by our email service provider. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of any email, or by writing to info@frostivaltexas.com.

Text messages

If you check the box to receive texts, we collect your mobile phone number and your consent to receive recurring automated marketing text messages from Frostival, such as ticket drops, special offers and event announcements. We may also send event-day reminders, weather closures and schedule updates.

Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. You can also contact us at info@frostivaltexas.com.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Mobile Terms of Service for the full text message program terms.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as described here:

  • Service providers who help us run Frostival, such as our email and text messaging platform, ticketing provider, website hosting and analytics and advertising partners. They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when required by law or to protect the rights, property or safety of Frostival, our guests or others.
  • Business changes, if Frostival or Eastover, LLC is involved in a merger, sale or transfer of assets, in which case your information may transfer as part of that deal.

These sharing practices never include your mobile opt-in data or text messaging consent, as stated in Text messages.

Cookies & analytics

We and our partners may use cookies, pixels and similar tools to keep the website working, measure traffic and understand which of our ads and emails are useful. Some of these tools may be operated by third parties such as analytics or social media advertising platforms. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work, though some features may not.

Other websites

Our website links to and loads content from other services, including our ticketing site, map and directions services, social media pages and web font providers. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you stay on our list or as needed to provide our services, then for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting and record-keeping requirements. If you unsubscribe, we keep a record of that choice so we don't contact you again.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails using the link in any email.
  • Stop texts by replying STOP to any message.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@frostivaltexas.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws, and we will honor them as required. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we may keep information we're required to keep by law.

Children

Frostival is a family event, but our website and mailing lists are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No website or transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website or sent to you by email.
